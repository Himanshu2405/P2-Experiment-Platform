# demo-banner: Conclusion

- **Decision:** Ship
- **DS:** Priya (fictional)
- **Date of report:** 2026-10-04

## Executive summary

The free-shipping banner raised the conversion rate from 4.30% to 5.60% (+1.30 pp, +30.2% relative), a significant improvement (p < 0.001). The refund guardrail passed, the split was balanced and the placebo A/A check passed. We recommend shipping the banner to all shoppers with items in the cart.

## Key findings

- **Primary metric:** conversion rate rose 30.2%, from 4.30% to 5.60%. The 95% interval for the difference is +0.70 pp to +1.90 pp, so even the low end is a real gain. The lift matches the 30% we planned for.
- **More orders per shopper:** orders per user rose 28.6% (p < 0.001), so the extra conversions did not come from smaller, more frequent baskets.
- **Guardrail passed:** the refund rate moved from 0.61% to 0.66%, well inside the allowance of up to double the baseline.
- **Checks passed:** the split was balanced (p = 19.3%) and the placebo A/A check found a false difference 6.5% of the time, inside the normal range for a 5% test.

## Notes on the results

Add-to-cart rate moved down from 12.46% to 11.61% (-6.8%), which is not significant (p = 0.064). The banner only appears once an item is in the cart, so we do not expect it to change add-to-cart, and we treat this as noise. We will keep watching it after launch.

The refund allowance in the plan (up to double the baseline) was generous. The observed rise is small, so it does not change the decision, but a tighter allowance would be better for future tests.

## Recommendation

Ship the banner to 100% of shoppers with items in the cart. Watch add-to-cart rate and refund rate for two weeks after launch. For the next free-shipping test, set a tighter refund guardrail.
