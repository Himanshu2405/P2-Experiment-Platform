# Email: product context

All facts in this file are fictional. The product is simulated.

## What it is

Email is the campaign tool of a fictional company: it sends newsletters and offers to readers, who open, click, unsubscribe or bounce.

## The funnel

1. **Send:** a campaign email is sent to a reader.
2. **Open:** the reader opens the email.
3. **Click:** the reader clicks a link in the email.
4. **Unsubscribe or bounce:** the reader leaves the list, or the email cannot be delivered.

Each reader is measured from the day they were assigned to the last day of the test.

## Key metrics

| Metric | Definition | Better when |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate | Share of readers who opened at least one email | Higher |
| Click rate | Share of readers who clicked at least one email | Higher |
| Emails per user | Average number of emails sent to a reader | Neither (it shows the sending, not the reader) |
| Unsubscribe rate | Share of readers who unsubscribed | Lower |
| Bounce rate | Share of readers with at least one bounced email | Lower |

## Who is in the list

Readers are on free or paid plans and live in several regions. Some are new to the list and some have been on it for years.

## Typical numbers

About 38% of readers open, about 5% click, and well under 1% unsubscribe in three weeks. Traffic is higher than checkout: tens of thousands of readers a month in the simulated data.

## Words we use

- **Reader:** a person on the email list, also called a user in the metrics.
- **Lift:** the relative change of the variant against control.
- **Guardrail:** a metric we must not make worse.
